\section{Judgment-induced safety disagreement and cross-domain oracle budgets}

We evaluate disagreement with the available pooled TREC DL 2019 judgments,
denoted FULL, without interpreting that pool as exhaustive relevance truth.
For query $q$ and budget $e$, let the loss be
$L(q,e)=\max(0,\mathrm{nDCG}_{\rm exact}@10-\mathrm{nDCG}_{e}@10)$.
A false-safe instance satisfies $L_{\rm FULL}>\epsilon$ and
$L_{\rm thin}\leq\epsilon$, holding rankings and work fixed.
The conditional masking fraction divides by FULL-unsafe instances, whereas
the all-pair false-safe rate divides by every tested query--budget instance.

There are 43 queries and ten efSearch budgets. At $\epsilon=0.025$,
14 of 430 FULL pairs are unsafe. Across 50 paired deterministic thinning
seeds, 50\%, 25\%, and one-positive-per-query retention mask respectively
$192/700=27.43\%$, $351/700=50.14\%$, and $608/700=86.86\%$ of
FULL-unsafe instances. The corresponding all-pair rates are 0.893\%,
1.633\%, and 2.828\%. At 50\% retention, 183 of 192 false-safe instances
(95.31\%) retain positive exact-reference nDCG under thinning. These
dependent instances do not constitute independent Bernoulli trials.
At tolerances 0.01 and 0.05, the one-positive masking fractions remain
86.86\% and 82.20\%, respectively; FULL-unsafe denominators are 14 and 10
pairs. All 64,930 saved scoring rows reproduce independently with zero
score or ANN-recall error. Frozen rankings, selections, and work are unchanged.

Separately, recomputing FiQA oracle minima from saved query--budget records
matches all 648 saved oracle rows. Of 305 exact-positive queries, 303 have
both feasible minima under ANN Recall@10 $\geq0.90$ and nDCG loss
$\leq0.025$. Mean ANN-safe and IR-safe efSearch are 24.086 and 15.525,
giving a mean gap of 8.561 ef units; IR requires a lower, equal, or higher
budget for 120, 166, or 17 queries. A retrospective paired-query percentile
bootstrap with 10,000 resamples and PCG64 seed 42 reproduces the supplied
95\% interval $[5.498,11.624]$. The original interval-generating script was
not recovered. A separately declared 50,000-resample sensitivity with seed
20261126 gives $[5.531,11.584]$. Infeasible minima remain missing.

FiQA replicates oracle ANN-versus-IR budget mismatch in another domain;
it does not replicate judgment-density false safety or establish CURA
controller transfer. These diagnostics also do not show that CURA universally
beats fixed HNSW: the calibration-selected fixed baseline can be beaten,
while a post-hoc risk-matched fixed budget can be cheaper than the current
CURA implementation. The research question concerns when utility-aware
budgets deliver genuine gains and how judgment completeness and risk
calibration affect those gains.
