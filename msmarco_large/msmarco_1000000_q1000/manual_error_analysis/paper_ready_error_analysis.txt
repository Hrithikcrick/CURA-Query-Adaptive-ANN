We manually reviewed 50 high-priority queries selected from CURA's held-out evaluation, including 47 MS MARCO test queries and 3 TREC-DL-2019 out-of-distribution queries. The most common manually assigned categories were IR_FAILURE_DESPITE_HIGH_ANN_RECALL (4/50, 8.0%), EARLY_STOP_TOO_AGGRESSIVE (5/50, 10.0%), LONG_OR_COMPLEX_QUERY (11/50, 22.0%). Among the reviewed cases, 3 queries were actual downstream IR violations under the predefined nDCG-loss criterion. The dominant categories among these actual failures were IR_FAILURE_DESPITE_HIGH_ANN_RECALL (2), EARLY_STOP_TOO_AGGRESSIVE (1). This analysis indicates that CURA's residual errors are not uniformly distributed across queries, and helps identify the query types for which downstream-risk estimation remains most difficult.
