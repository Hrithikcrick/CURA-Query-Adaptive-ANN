Scientific interpretation:
Most selected failures (34/50) have ANN recall below 0.90. Incomplete ANN reconstruction therefore remains a common numerical correlate, including rows given more specific primary labels.
Nevertheless, 16/50 have recall >= 0.90 (all are 0.90 in this snapshot). Among these, 13 have zero approximate nDCG@10 and 5 have nDCG loss exactly 1. This is an observed counterexample to treating high neighbor overlap as a sufficient guarantee of downstream IR safety. It does not establish performance at recall above 0.90.
Small exact rank-10/11 boundaries occur in 19/50 cases. 17 receive the provisional NEAR_TIE_BOUNDARY primary label, while two high-recall cases retain the higher-priority disagreement label. A small boundary is consistent with cutoff sensitivity, but aggregate metrics cannot establish which relevant documents were missed or whether the boundary caused the utility loss.
Semantic labels describe plausible query properties. No entity-frequency measurements or retrieved-document content were used; entity rarity and causal retrieval difficulty have not been established.

Selection and interpretation limits:
12_prepare_50_actual_failures.py first chooses the highest-ANN-recall unsafe budget per query (ties: larger loss, then smaller efSearch). It prioritizes high-recall failures, TREC cases, small boundaries, then severe failures. This deliberately enriched selection is not random.
The 32% high-recall coexistence statistic and all category shares describe these 50 selected cases only. They are neither population failure-prevalence estimates nor final CURA-policy failure rates, and do not demonstrate a calibrated CURA advantage.

Reproducibility and annotation protocol:
Only existing CSVs are read. No encoding, indexing, search, or HNSW experiment is run. Python standard library only; semantic judgments and review flags are frozen in 18_annotate_50_actual_failures.py.
Primary priority: high recall/IR disagreement; provisional numerical boundary feature; clear semantic reading; distribution shift when supported; generic low recovery; other.
HIGH means the descriptive numerical label follows directly from the recorded metrics. MEDIUM means a plausible boundary feature or semantic interpretation, with causation unverified. LOW means a competing explanation or unresolved referent requires researcher judgment. Confidence is qualitative, not a calibrated probability.
Six boundary cases are explicitly flagged LOW because very poor neighbor recovery competes strongly with the cutoff interpretation. Two ambiguous entity/work cases are LOW. These are frozen qualitative review judgments, not an additional experimental threshold. Other boundary labels are MEDIUM for the observed feature only.
The legacy numeric_suggestion value OOD_FAILURE is preserved but is not a valid output primary category. Manual fields and review_complete are copied unchanged; new fields do not certify human review.
Empty-category means are blank (undefined), not fabricated zeros. Summary means are derived using Decimal and displayed to 12 decimal places; original numeric strings remain exact.
