# Revised SIGIR research direction

**Research question:** When do downstream-utility-aware ANN search budgets provide genuine efficiency gains, and how are those gains affected by relevance-judgment completeness and risk calibration?

**Current direction:** analyze annotation-sensitive oracle headroom together with fixed-budget calibration and the cost of adaptive implementation. The recovered same-ranking TREC experiment supports **Outcome A**: thinning positive judgments increases the measured ANN-minus-IR safe-budget gap. The preserved fixed-frontier result still shows that this CURA implementation has not established an adaptive efficiency advantage.

## Claims supported by the evidence

1. **Metric-dependent oracle opportunity persists on exact-positive MS MARCO queries.** The 812 jointly feasible pairs have an ANN-minus-IR mean gap of 7.611 ef (historical paired-query 95% CI [5.933, 9.387]). Keep grid-infeasibility flags and capped-estimator sensitivity explicit. ef differences do not establish distance-work savings.
2. **The original saving is not purely an exact-zero artifact.** The 159 exact-positive test queries contribute 92.59% of the total work saving against calibration-selected ef96. Their saving is 23.09% (CI [13.91, 31.58]%). This rejects the narrow zero-reference explanation, not sparse-judgment effects within positive references.
3. **Judgment thinning increases apparent budget headroom on unchanged rankings.** FULL gives mean gap -0.140 ef; 50%, 25%, and one-positive conditions give 0.308, 0.848, and 1.956. All three stochastic conditions have 50 completed paired seeds. ANN-safe mean ef remains 12.279, while mean IR-safe ef falls from 12.419 to 10.323 in the one-positive condition. The sequence describes condition means, not universal query/seed monotonicity.
4. **The reference criterion becomes degenerate under extreme thinning.** Exact-zero nDCG rises from 0% under FULL to 82.88% with one positive per query. Nonnegative approximate nDCG then implies zero one-sided reference loss automatically. Increased measured headroom is not evidence of improved retrieval or actual relevance preservation.
5. **The complete fixed frontier remains negative for current CURA.** Post-hoc fixed ef48 matches CURA's observed 2/200 test violations with 28.29% less work; fixed ef64 has lower observed risk and work. On TREC under FULL, fixed ef32 matches 1/43 violations with 53.48% less work. Retrospective matching is a diagnostic, not a prospectively calibrated replacement or proof of equal population risk. Charge probe plus fresh selected-search work; this is two-stage allocation, not integrated traversal exit.

Together these claims support an analysis of **when measured oracle opportunity survives annotation choices and when adaptive implementation justifies its cost**. They do not support a universal adaptive-retrieval win. A stronger algorithmic claim would require independently validated full-cost improvement over a properly selected fixed risk/work frontier.

## Recovery and FULL reproduction

Original exact and HNSW ranked IDs were recovered directly from Downloads/IR_PROJECT.zip, under IR_PROJECT/PILOT/, using the original ordered map. Seven archived run/source files match the saved experiment byte-for-byte. Exact top100 rankings are available for all 43 TREC queries; HNSW top10 rankings cover all ten ef settings. HNSW score evidence was reconstructed from original cached vectors without altering original rank order. The index was not loaded or searched. No encoding, index construction, retrieval or controller training was performed.

The strict FULL gate passed 43 exact and 430 HNSW query-budget comparisons before thinning evaluation: nDCG/loss tolerance 1e-6, ANN Recall tolerance 1e-9, and identical violation indicators. Preserve this limitation in the manuscript.

## Density design and inference

FULL means all available pooled qrels, not complete ground truth. Positive-only thinning retains original grades and all nonpositive judgments; removed positives become unjudged. Nested per-query permutation prefixes retain max(1,ceil(p*n)) for p=0.5 or 0.25, or one positive. Recompute both exact and approximate graded nDCG and IDCG from retained judgments under the original unjudged=0 convention.

Rankings, ANN Recall, measured work/timing, thresholds and CURA choices are invariant. All 151 qrel sets match the previously prepared hashes, and all 43 queries remain jointly feasible under both criteria in every condition/seed. The [summary](../sigir_analysis/trec_judgment_density_summary.csv), [per-seed results](../sigir_analysis/trec_judgment_density_per_seed.csv), [fixed frontiers](../sigir_analysis/trec_fixed_risk_work_by_density.csv), [frozen-policy results](../sigir_analysis/trec_cura_by_density.csv) and [promotion validation](../sigir_analysis/density_promotion_validation.json) contain the actual observations.

The gap changes relative to FULL are 0.447 ef (95% CI [0.300, 0.593]), 0.988 ([0.820, 1.153]) and 2.096 ([2.001, 2.180]). These intervals resample **50 paired seed effects conditional on the fixed 43 queries and rankings**, not the 64,930 query-budget rows. No query-population or deployment guarantee follows, and no crossed query/seed bootstrap was performed. Thinning tests one annotation mechanism; DCG and IDCG can both change.

The 12 completed density comparisons cover gap (P5), exact-zero fraction (P6), IR-safe ef (P7), and IR-allows-lower rate (P8), each at three densities. P7 is an exact sign reversal of P5, not independent corroboration. Holm correction covers those 12 dependent follow-ups only; CIs are unadjusted. Previously completed P1-P4 and all 42 completed historical statistical rows were preserved rather than rerun. See [statistical scope](../sigir_analysis/statistical_analysis_summary.md), [density results](../sigir_analysis/density_statistical_tests_completed.csv) and [statistical validation](../sigir_analysis/density_statistics_validation.json).
